# Institution marks

Five university logos identify the authors' affiliations and link to official university websites. Original asset URLs are recorded in [people.json](../../data/people.json).

Marks are cropped and scaled proportionally. HUST, Tsinghua, WHUT and Toronto foregrounds are recolored for legibility; Lehigh retains its original colors.

Logos belong to their respective universities and do not imply endorsement.
